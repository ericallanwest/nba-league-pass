    Notes & sources
    • Blackout teams come from NBA.com's League Pass ZIP lookup (the form on nba.com/league-pass-purchase), queried for every ZIP. Check NBA.com for the authoritative answer for your address.
    • Points are the Census Bureau's 2025 ZIP Code Tabulation Areas (TIGER/Line), plotted at each area's internal point. ZCTAs approximate USPS ZIP codes, and PO-box-only ZIPs aren't included.
    • Town and state are the USPS city name for each ZIP (via the zipcodes package). Population is from the Census Bureau's American Community Survey.
    • Closest teams are ranked by typical drive time without traffic, from openrouteservice (© OpenStreetMap contributors), from each ZIP's center point to the arena. Miles are driving distance by the same route. Until a ZIP's drive times are computed, and for ZIPs with no road route to an arena (Hawaii), the list shows the nearest arenas in a straight line, labeled as such.
    • A team color marks a ZIP blacked out for that team alone. A ZIP blacked out for two or more teams is gray with a darker outline, even when one of them is selected; under Multiple it takes a selected team's color instead, with a heavier black ring if two or more of the selected teams are blacked out there. One and Multiple count all of a ZIP's blacked-out teams, not just the selected ones; None shows ZIPs with no local blackout.
    • Rings show a 75-mile radius around each selected team's arena, for comparison with its blackout area.
    • Gray dots are ZIPs outside the current filter. A darker outline means two or more teams are blacked out there; no outline means one team or none.
    • About 7,000 USPS ZIP codes aren't Census ZIP areas: PO-box sections, single organizations (a university, a company, a federal agency) and ZIPs created after the 2020 Census. They aren't drawn, but the ZIP search finds them and shows NBA.com's blackouts for each, at the Census ZIP area they sit in. Sources: HRSA ZIP Code to ZCTA Crosswalk; USPS names via the zipcodes package.
    • Alaska, Puerto Rico and the other U.S. territories aren't shown. None of their ZIPs is blacked out: NBA.com lists no local teams for any Alaska ZIP and doesn't recognize ZIPs in Puerto Rico or the territories. A handful of ZIPs elsewhere that NBA.com doesn't recognize show as gray with no outline.
    • Nationally televised games have their own blackouts, which this map doesn't cover.
