NBA League Pass Blackout Map

Where each team's games are blacked out on League Pass because a local broadcaster holds the rights.

    Notes & sources
    • Blackout teams come from NBA.com's League Pass ZIP lookup (the form on nba.com/league-pass-purchase), queried for every ZIP. Check NBA.com for the authoritative answer for your address.
    • Points are the Census Bureau's 2025 ZIP Code Tabulation Areas (TIGER/Line), plotted at each area's internal point. ZCTAs approximate USPS ZIP codes, and PO-box-only ZIPs aren't included.
    • Town and state are the USPS city name for each ZIP (via the zipcodes package). Population is from the Census Bureau's American Community Survey.
    • Closest teams are ranked by typical drive time without traffic, from openrouteservice (© OpenStreetMap contributors), from each ZIP's center point to the arena. Miles are straight-line. ZIPs with no road route to an arena (Alaska, Hawaii, territories) list the nearest arenas in a straight line.
    • A ZIP ringed in black is blacked out for two or more of the selected teams.
    • Puerto Rico and a few other ZIPs aren't recognized by NBA.com, so they show as no data.
    • Nationally televised games have their own blackouts, which this map doesn't cover.
