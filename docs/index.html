    Notes & sources
    • Blackouts come from NBA.com's League Pass ZIP lookup (nba.com/league-pass-purchase), queried for every ZIP. Check NBA.com for the final word on your address. National TV blackouts are in the TV Schedule panel.
    • Points are the Census Bureau's 2025 ZIP Code Tabulation Areas, at each area's internal point, plus 203 USPS ZIPs created since the 2020 Census, placed with HUD's USPS ZIP crosswalk. About 7,000 other USPS ZIPs (PO-box sections, single organizations) have no area of their own; they aren't drawn, but the search finds them (HRSA crosswalk).
    • Population is from the Census Bureau's American Community Survey; for the newer ZIPs it's estimated from Census tracts. The map starts at 500+ people; drag the slider to 0 to see every ZIP. "PO Boxes Only" marks ZIPs with no residents because they serve only post office boxes.
    • Town and state are the USPS city name (zipcodes package). State lines are the Census Bureau's 2025 cartographic boundaries, simplified.
    • Colors: a team color marks a ZIP blacked out for that team alone. A ZIP blacked out for several teams is a pie, one equal slice per team, each turned toward its arena (Knicks/Nets and Lakers/Clippers split north/south). One and Multiple count all of a ZIP's teams, not just the selected ones. Teal marks ZIPs with no data: NBA.com lists no local teams for them (or doesn't recognize them), though some are likely blacked out all the same.
    • Rings show each team's 75-mile marketing territory, which the NBA measures from the home city's limits: the smallest circle holding every point within 75 miles of them (Census 2025 city boundaries; Toronto from OpenStreetMap; San Francisco without the Farallon Islands). They show for the selected teams, or for every team when none is selected.
    • Closest teams are ranked by typical drive time without traffic, by road from each ZIP's nearest road point (openrouteservice, © OpenStreetMap contributors), or by straight-line distance to the arena (Closest teams by). Where drive times aren't computed yet, or there's no road route (Hawaii), drive time falls back to straight line.
    • Not shown: Alaska, where NBA.com lists no local teams for any ZIP, and Puerto Rico and the other territories, whose ZIPs it doesn't recognize.
    • Data last built —. Code and data: github.com/ericallanwest/nba-league-pass.

    TV Schedule

    League Pass doesn't show live games on ESPN, ABC, NBC, Peacock or Prime Video anywhere in the US, so how many of a team's 82 games you can watch depends on how often it plays on national TV. NBA TV games stay on League Pass.
