League Pass national blackouts

League Pass doesn't show live games on ESPN, ABC, NBC, Peacock or Prime Video anywhere in the US. So even outside a team's local blackout area, how many of its 82 games you can watch live depends on how often it plays on national TV. NBA TV games stay on League Pass.

    • Counts are for a viewer outside the team's local blackout area. Inside it, every game is blacked out on League Pass.
    • Each team's last two games depend on the NBA Cup group stage and are added once the league schedules them in December.
